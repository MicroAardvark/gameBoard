GAME OBJECTIVE:
Be the first player to get rid of all your cards. When you have only one card left, you must call "Crazy Eights!" or face a penalty.

BASIC RULES:

On your turn, you must play a card that matches either:
- The suit of the top card (e.g., Hearts on Hearts)
- The rank of the top card (e.g., 7 on 7)

If you cannot play a card, you must draw one card from the draw pile. If the drawn card can be played, you may play it immediately. Otherwise, your turn ends.

SPECIAL CARDS - ENHANCED RULES:

JACKS - Dual Choice Power:
When you play a Jack, choose one of two actions
- Skip the next player's turn, OR
- Reverse the direction of play

QUEENS - Stackable Draw Two:
The next player must draw 2 cards unless they also play a Queen. Queens can be stacked! If Player 2 plays a Queen on Player 1's Queen, Player 3 must draw 4 cards (or stack another Queen for 6, and so on).

KINGS - Suit Change:
Choose any suit. The next player must play that suit or another King.

ACES - Wild Draw Four:
The ultimate power card! Choose any suit AND the next player draws 4 cards. Aces can also be stacked like Queens, forcing the next player to draw 8, 12, or even more cards!

NUMBER CARDS:
Play normally by matching suit or rank. No special effects.

CRAZY EIGHTS RULE:

When you have exactly one card remaining, you MUST call "Crazy Eights!" before the next player takes their turn.

- If you call it correctly: Continue playing normally
- If you forget: Draw 2 penalty cards as punishment

Other players can challenge you if they believe you failed to call "Crazy Eights!" in time.

WINNING THE GAME:

The first player to successfully play all their cards wins! If you win by playing a special card (Jack, Queen, King, or Ace), its effect still applies to the other players - even though you've won!
